No-Bake Italian Dessert

Classic Tiramisu

Espresso-soaked ladyfingers layered with a rich, creamy mascarpone mixture and dusted with cocoa — smooth, coffee-kissed, and made to chill overnight.

Serves8
Active time35 min
Chill time4–12 hrs

Ingredients

  • 4large egg yolks
  • 1/2 cupgranulated sugar
  • 16 ozmascarpone cheese
  • 1 cupheavy cream
  • 1 1/2 cupsstrong brewed espresso, cooled
  • 2 tbspcoffee liqueur (optional)
  • 24ladyfinger cookies (savoiardi)
  • 2 tbspunsweetened cocoa powder, for dusting

Instructions

  1. Whisk yolks and sugar

    In a heatproof bowl, whisk the egg yolks and sugar together until pale and thick.

  2. Cook the yolk mixture

    Set the bowl over a pot of simmering water and whisk constantly until thickened, about 5–8 minutes.

    7 min

  3. Cool, then fold in mascarpone

    Let the mixture cool to room temperature, then whisk in the mascarpone until smooth.

  4. Whip the cream

    Whip the heavy cream to stiff peaks in a separate bowl, then gently fold it into the mascarpone mixture.

  5. Dip and layer ladyfingers

    Combine the espresso and coffee liqueur. Quickly dip each ladyfinger (1–2 seconds per side, don’t soak) and arrange a layer in a 9×13″ dish.

  6. Layer and repeat

    Spread half the mascarpone mixture over the ladyfingers, add a second dipped layer, then top with the rest of the cream.

  7. Chill

    Cover and refrigerate at least 4 hours, ideally overnight, so the layers set.

    4+ hrs

  8. Dust and serve

    Just before serving, dust generously with cocoa powder. Slice and enjoy.

Tip: For an alcohol-free version, skip the coffee liqueur and use a little extra espresso. Tiramisu tastes best the next day, once the ladyfingers have softened and the flavors have melded.
